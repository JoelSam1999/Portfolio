01 — Approach

Three methods I trust, chosen to fit you.

Most of my work draws on CBT, Transactional Analysis and Gestalt therapy. We use whichever helps most with what you're facing.

CBT

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy · Certified 2023

We map how your thoughts, feelings and actions feed each other, then build practical skills to break unhelpful loops. Well suited to anxiety, overthinking and low mood.

In sessionIs this thought a fact, or a habit?

Transactional Analysis

TA-101, International TA Association · 2022

We look at the roles and scripts you fall into with partners, family and colleagues, and practise responding as the adult you are today. Useful for relationships and recurring conflict.

In sessionWhich part of me is reacting right now?

Gestalt

Gestalt psychotherapy

We slow down and notice what is happening now, in your body, your words and the room, so that unfinished feelings can be felt and resolved rather than avoided.

In sessionWhat are you aware of, right now?
